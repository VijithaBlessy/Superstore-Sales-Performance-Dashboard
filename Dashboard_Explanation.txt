SUPERSTORE SALES PERFORMANCE DASHBOARD

1. INTRODUCTION

The Superstore Sales Performance Dashboard is created using Microsoft Power BI. It helps to analyze sales performance based on region, product category, and month.

2. KEY PERFORMANCE INDICATORS

Total Sales:
Displays the overall sales revenue.

Total Profit:
Shows the total profit generated from sales.

Total Quantity:
Displays the total quantity of products sold.

3. VISUALIZATIONS

Sales by Region:
This chart compares sales across four regions: West, East, Central, and South. It helps identify the highest-performing region.

Sales by Category:
This chart compares sales across Technology, Furniture, and Office Supplies categories. Technology has the highest sales.

Monthly Sales Trend:
This line chart shows sales variations over time and helps understand monthly sales performance.

4. INTERACTIVE FILTERS

Region Filter:
Allows users to select a specific region.

Category Filter:
Allows users to select a specific product category.

The charts and KPI cards update automatically when filters are selected.

5. KEY INSIGHTS

1. West region has the highest overall sales.
2. Technology is the highest-selling category.
3. Sales performance changes across different months.

6. TOOLS USED

Microsoft Power BI Desktop
Sample Superstore Dataset

7. CONCLUSION

This dashboard provides a simple and interactive way to understand sales performance. It helps users compare regions, analyze product categories, and understand monthly sales trends.
